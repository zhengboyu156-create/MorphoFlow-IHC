# Experimental RGB flow and PatchNCE

RGB is normalized to [-1,1]. The base flow regresses Gaussian-noise-to-IHC velocity while conditioned on the **same IHC training crop**. Region prediction is supervised by its own IHC DAB proxy. For an independent H&E crop, a short differentiable Euler rollout generates RGB from noise, conditioned on that H&E and its predicted region probabilities. Same-position features extracted from H&E and the generated image are positives in PatchNCE, other positions within the image are negatives. This is one weighted structure objective in addition to flow and region objectives. `nce_steps` sets the short training rollout, which can differ from `ode_steps` at inference.

At inference RGB is directly output and overlapping tile RGB predictions are blended. The output is not assembled by compositing a copied H&E hematoxylin channel with DAB. Its optional DAB diagnostic is extracted from the generated RGB after inference.

The trajectory policy operates at a single Euler step on RGB velocity. For training its bounded terminal reward still compares the generated DAB proxy against the DAB in the same IHC conditioning crop. This signal is not a matched H&E biomarker label and cannot establish biomarker prediction for H&E. An H&E–IHC domain gap and an underdetermined unpaired mapping remain. The original DAB model, its caches and checkpoints are incompatible with this version; consult commit 75942f8 for the prior code.
